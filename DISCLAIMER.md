## Phase 3 — Deterministic Backtest Engine

This phase introduces a deterministic backtest engine that replaces mock metrics with real computed performance metrics.

- **Backtest results are historical simulations, not predictions**: The deterministic backtest engine calculates performance metrics from historical OHLC data using a Moving Average Crossover strategy. These results are educational simulations only and do not guarantee future market performance.
- **No fabricated metrics**: All performance metrics (win rate, average win, average loss, profit factor, maximum drawdown) originate from deterministic computation over historical data. No metrics are invented, hard-coded, or fabricated.
- **Parameter disclosure**: The 20/50 SMA strategy parameters are demonstration values, not claimed optimal or recommended parameters.
- **Transaction costs are assumptions**: Commission (0.1%) and slippage (0.05%) are simulation assumptions, not broker-specific rates.
- **Historical performance does not guarantee future results**: Past simulated results in this educational platform do not predict future market outcomes.
- **Risk controls are educational simulation**: The risk controls inspired by SEBI principles are educational simulation only, not regulatory compliance.
